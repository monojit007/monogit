# SBI Scale 2 to Scale 3 — Promotion Study Notes

These notes explain the Web Technologies and Emerging Technologies portion of the Scale 2 to Scale 3 syllabus in plain language.

Read them as a student who is seeing these words for the first time. Every chapter starts with a story, then explains each word, then shows how a bank uses the idea, then ends with lines worth remembering for the exam.

## How to study

1. Read one chapter in one sitting. Do not rush to the next chapter.
2. After each chapter, close the file and say the story out loud in your own words.
3. Then open the chapter again and check the "Say it in one line" boxes.
4. After all chapters, use `11-revision-sheet.md` for a fast second reading.
5. Use `12-word-by-word-glossary.md` when a single word confuses you.

## A picture to keep in your head

Think of a customer opening the bank app on a phone.

1. The **front end** is what the customer sees and touches: screens, buttons, colours, messages.
2. The **back end** is the office behind the screen: it checks the customer, talks to the core banking system, and prepares the answer.
3. The **architecture** is the building plan: who sits where, and how the rooms connect.
4. **Security** is the lock, the guard, and the rule that only the right person may enter.
5. **APIs** are the official service windows through which one system asks another system for help.
6. **Performance and scalability** mean the app stays fast when one customer uses it and when one lakh customers use it together.
7. **Hosting and cloud** mean where the computers that run the app actually live.
8. **Generative AI, Big Data, Blockchain, IoT, Edge, RPA, and Quantum** are newer tools. A senior manager must know what each one is for, where it helps a bank, and where it can cause harm.
